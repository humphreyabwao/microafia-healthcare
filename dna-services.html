Sample Collection Information

What We Collect

We collect mouth swabs (buccal swabs) - a painless procedure that takes just a few seconds. For prenatal tests, we collect mother's blood and father's mouth swab.

Collection Options

  • Visit our clinic in Nairobi
  • Free home sample collection
  • We can send swab kits to you

Requirements

  • Valid ID for all participants
  • Consent from all parties
  • Father's consent for prenatal
