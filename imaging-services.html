Our Ultrasound Services

Comprehensive diagnostic imaging for all your healthcare needs

Service Price (KSh) Clinical Indications
Obstetric (ANC) Scan 1,500 Confirm dating, viability, fetal growth, anomaly screening, placental position, and ectopic suspicion.
Abdominal Scan 1,500 Investigate persistent pain, evaluate liver/gallbladder pathology (e.g., gallstones), spleen, and pancreas.
Pelvic Scan (Female) 1,500 Assess abnormal bleeding, chronic pain, diagnose fibroids, ovarian cysts, and check IUCD position.
KUBP Scan (K/U/B/Prostate) 2,000 Work-up hematuria/flank pain, detect kidney/bladder stones, blockages, and check the prostate (male).
Prostate Scan 2,000 Investigate LUTS (urinary symptoms), BPH assessment, and check for prostatic masses.
Breast Scan 2,000 Characterize palpable lumps (solid vs. cyst), evaluate dense breast tissue, and mastitis.
Testicular/Scrotal Scan 2,000 Investigate scrotal pain, swelling, masses (e.g., hydrocele, varicocele), or potential torsion.
Thyroid Scan 2,000 Evaluate thyroid nodules (size/risk), goitre (enlargement), and investigate abnormal function tests.
Regional/MSK Scan 2,000 Assess joint effusions, tendon tears (e.g., rotator cuff), ligament sprains, and soft tissue masses.
Doppler U/S (Lower Limb) 3,500 Diagnose Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT), assess varicose veins, and evaluate lower limb arterial flow.
Doppler U/S (Neck/Carotid) 3,500 Assess stroke risk by checking for narrowing (stenosis) or plaques in the carotid arteries.

Prices are subject to change. Please contact us for the most current pricing and to schedule your appointment.
