Choose your scans

Prices include the scan and a written report.

  • Obstetric (ANC) scan

    Confirms dating and viability, checks baby's growth and placenta position, screens for anomalies and suspected ectopic pregnancy.

    KSh 1,500

  • Pelvic scan (female)

    Assesses abnormal bleeding and chronic pelvic pain, diagnoses fibroids and ovarian cysts, and checks IUCD position.

    KSh 1,500

  • Breast scan

    Tells solid lumps from cysts, evaluates dense breast tissue and investigates mastitis.

    KSh 2,000

  • Abdominal scan

    Investigates persistent abdominal pain and checks the liver, gallbladder (e.g. gallstones), spleen and pancreas.

    KSh 1,500

  • KUB / KUBP scan

    Kidneys, ureters and bladder (plus prostate in men). Works up blood in urine and flank pain, and detects stones or blockages.

    KSh 2,000

  • Prostate scan

    Investigates urinary symptoms, assesses an enlarged prostate (BPH) and checks for prostatic masses.

    KSh 2,000

  • Testicular / scrotal scan

    Investigates scrotal pain, swelling and masses such as hydrocele or varicocele, and possible torsion.

    KSh 2,000

  • Thyroid scan

    Evaluates thyroid nodules and goitre, and follows up abnormal thyroid function tests.

    KSh 2,000

  • Regional / MSK scan

    Assesses joint swelling, tendon tears (e.g. rotator cuff), ligament sprains and soft-tissue lumps.

    KSh 2,000

  • Doppler scan (lower limb)

    Diagnoses deep vein thrombosis (DVT), assesses varicose veins and checks arterial blood flow in the legs.

    KSh 3,500

  • Doppler scan (neck / carotid)

    Assesses stroke risk by checking the carotid arteries for narrowing or plaque.

    KSh 3,500
